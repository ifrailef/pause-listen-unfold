This work explores the space between technical competence and artistic presence.

Embodied Musicianship is an invitation to reconnect with the intelligence of the body and allow music to arise from a place that feels natural, alive, and authentic. Rather than focusing only on technique, we explore presence, awareness, and the relationship between the musician and their instrument.

When we learn to listen beneath our habits, fears, and expectations, expression becomes less about trying to perform and more about allowing what is already there to be heard. The instrument becomes a vehicle rather than the source of creativity.

These sessions are open to musicians of any instrument or level who wish to cultivate a deeper connection with themselves, their creativity, and the way they express their unique voice through music.

About

We are governors of ourselves. We’d simply forgotten.

I'm Irene.

I come from a formal scientific education, where I learned to value curiosity, observation, and the search for understanding. In my early twenties, after following a formal educational way, a series of life experiences changed the way I understood the world. For the first time, I realised that not everything I had been taught was necessarily true, and that I was free to question and explore my own way of relating to life.

Since then, I've been following what I can only describe as a quiet inner current—a subtle but persistent sense of direction that has led me through life.

Now, everything I offer grows from this understanding. I've come to see that when we slow down enough to listen, we begin to notice a deeper intelligence already dwells within us. In this way, my work is not about providing answers or changing people. Instead, it is about creating the conditions in which greater potential and authentic expression can naturally unfold.
